Meet the

Imagine a tree that grows smaller branches from its branches. Recursion is just like that: a function solves a little problem by asking a smaller version of itself for help.

🌳 Grow the idea

Every recursive story has 3 parts

Think of recursion as a journey. It starts, gets smaller, reaches a stopping point, then brings answers back.

🛑

1. Base case

The stop sign! It tells the function, “That's small enough. We have an answer.”

🪄

2. Recursive call

The function asks a smaller version of itself to do a little job.

🎁

3. Return & unwind

When the smallest job is done, answers travel back up to the calls waiting for them.
